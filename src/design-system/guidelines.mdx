# Guidelines

<Lead>How Chilicon Power looks, speaks and behaves, in one page. When the app and this page disagree, the app is wrong.</Lead>

## The thesis

The app answers three questions in one second: **Is my system OK? What did it produce and save today? Which panel
needs attention?** The status card answers the first, hero numbers the second, the roof map the third.

## Platform

Apple HIG for structure and controls: large titles that collapse on scroll, a floating iOS 26 tab bar with labels
(max 4 tabs), sheets with a grabber and two detents, inset grouped lists in Profile, segmented controls for periods,
44 pt touch targets. Glass is used only on the floating tab bar; cards and content stay solid.

## Color

| Role | Tokens | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text | `ink`, `muted` | `muted` passes 4.5:1 on both `surface` and `canvas` |
| Actions | `brand`, `on-brand` | Primary buttons are the logo orange (#FBAB16) with dark text. Never grey primary buttons |
| Surfaces | `canvas`, `surface`, `raised`, `line` | Cards are `surface` on `canvas`, flat |
| Energy | `solar`, `home`, `battery`, `grid` | Always the same thing: solar is the logo orange, battery blue, grid violet, home slate |
| Status | `ok`, `warn`, `fault` | OK is the logo leaf green (#00AA79). Always with an icon and a word, never color alone |
| Text versions | `*-ink` | Light-theme fills fail AA as text; use the `-ink` step for words and numbers |

## Status vocabulary

| State | Tone | Icon | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OK | `ok` | check circle | All 24 panels producing |
| Needs attention | `warn` | alert triangle | Panel 7 is producing 40% less |
| Fault / offline | `fault` | wifi off / x circle | Gateway offline since 9:14 am |
| Low but normal | neutral | cloud | Cloudy day. Output is low across the whole roof |
| Night | neutral | moon | Night. Panels start producing around 6:12 am |

## Units

Power is **kW** (or **W** for one panel), energy is **kWh**. Lowercase k, capital W. Time is 12-hour US (`6:12 am`),
dates are `May 21`, money is USD. See Foundations → Units.

## Copy

- English, sentence case, plain words, active voice.
- Status answers first: "All panels producing", not "System status: nominal".
- Buttons say what happens: "Scan gateway", "Contact installer", "Restart device".
- Errors say what happened and what to do. No apologies.

## Adding a component

1. Props only, no store access. Colors, type and radius from tokens only.
2. A `*.stories.tsx` next to it, with every variant and state.
3. Check it in both themes with the toolbar switch.
